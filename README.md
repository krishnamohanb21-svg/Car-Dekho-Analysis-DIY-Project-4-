# Car-Dekho-Analysis-DIY-Project-4-

# Problem Statement of Car Dekho Analysis

Car Dekho's online used car market lists numerous used cars where sellers can create their listings with prices based on minimal knowledge of the market trends, making them either over-priced or under-priced leading to either customer mistrust in the car listings on the marketplace and further decreasing the pace of selling of vehicles. There is a necessity to perform an analysis of all the used car listings listed on the website of Car Dekho in the past which are expected to analyse what drives a particular car's selling price in the future-Age of vehicle, Brand of the vehicle, KM driven on the vehicle, Vehicle Fuel Type, Vehicle Gear box and Previous Owners

# Project Description of the Car Dekho Analysis

This project performs a Data Analysis (EDA) on the Car Dekho used car dataset, which contains the following attributes:
Car_Name : Name/model of the car
Year : Year of purchase
Selling_Price : Price at which the car is being sold (target variable, in lakhs)
Present_Price : Current ex-showroom price (in lakhs)
Kms_Driven : Total kilometers driven
Fuel_Type: Petrol / Diesel / CNG
Seller_Type: Dealer / Individual
Transmission: Manual / Automatic
Owner :Number of previous owners

# What is Data Wrangling ?

Data wrangling, a crucial phase in the data analysis process, involves preparing and cleaning raw data to make it suitable for analysis. This involves various tasks such as handling missing values, removing duplicates, and transforming data into a consistent format. Effective data wrangling ensures that the data is reliable and ready for exploration, enabling meaningful insights to be derived from it.


# What is the Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)

Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)is a vital phase in data analysis involving the examination and visualization of data to uncover patterns ,trends, and insights.in the context of an Airbnb data analysis project, EDA allows researchers to gain a Comprehensive understanding of the dataset's characteristics, distribution and relationships between variables. Through techniques such as summary stastics, data visualization ,and correlation analysis, EDA sets the stage for further investigation and hypothesis testing, uiding subsequent analytical decisions.

# Future Scope

1. Predictive modelling : Turn the EDA in to a complete ML pipeline, like Linear regression, Random Forest, XG Boost or Gradient Boosting. Develop a reliable pricing predictor. The model should no longer be descriptive.
2. Live price suggestions: Build into a webapp / API hosted onto the car Dekho car listing website that will enable sellers to instantly receive a reasonable estimation of a car, even while they enter details.
3. Large & richer data: We should feed more information to the model, such as car age, car condition, accident history, maintenance history, insurance validity, varying demand on a city-to-city or region to region basis, and time of year or market dynamics etc.. For more efficient vehicle valuation.
4. In-depth analysis at Brand/ Model level: Carry out analysis at a more detailed level for various Brands/ Models. Identify models where value retains or depreciation is too much. Such an information is useful for the business to launch a market campaign or managing stock.
5. Market Trends and Time Series analysis: Study the variation of market prices at different times across few months / years; study variation in demand, cycles, effect of introduction of new vehicles, or a trend away from the prevalent fuel types. Eg. Growing preference for Electric cars.
6. Geographic/ Regional analysis: Include region/ location information. Market prices may differ across the country due to variation in the price on account of taxes, demand, and other factors like road condition.
7. Integration with market data: While using internal data it is advisable to include competitor pricing from other market places. This enables a better estimation of the vehicle.
8. Consumer behavior analysis: The pricing data could be used in conjunction with buyer searches and purchase information to help advise customers what sort of car the could be considering purchase, given the stated budget or preferences (recommendation engine).
9. Detect anomaly/ fraud in listing: While creating an estimation model there may be cases wherein an estimated price is considerably higher/ lower than the actual, which may represent data entry mistakes or fraud listing. These discrepancies could be identified from the modeling output.
10. Auto dashboard generation: Develop an auto refreshing business dashboard using Power BI / Tableau, so that Car Dekho's business users may take better decisions based on market changes and pricing data, without needing manual recasting or redevelopment.

# Conclusion

This project was a study and interpretation of the Car Dekho used car dataset in order to identify and model those factors that affect the resale value of an automobile in the used car market. Through a comprehensive data cleaning process, analysis, and visualization, the project identified clear and understandable trends such as that an increase in the age of a car resulted in the depreciation of sale value, that increases in the distance travelled in the vehicle results in increased devaluation, and that differences in attributes such as fuel, transmission, seller type, and previous owners impact vehicle price. Most importantly, the analysis solidified that the original sale value of the vehicle and the age of the vehicle both represent the best and most powerful variables affecting resale value, while even additional influences such as previous owners and type of seller contribute significantly to price determination. Ultimately, the project showed how exploration of transaction data can replace speculation in evaluating the price of a used car and be the first step in providing value to consumers selling to or at Car Dekho and others in the used automobile market.
