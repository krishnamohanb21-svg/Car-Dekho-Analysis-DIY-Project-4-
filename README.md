# Car-Dekho-Analysis-DIY-Project-4-
Car Dekho's online used car market lists numerous used cars where sellers can create their listings with prices based on minimal knowledge of the market trends, making them either over-priced or under-priced leading to either customer mistrust in the car listings on the marketplace and further decreasing the pace of selling of vehicles. There is a necessity to perform an analysis of all the used car listings listed on the website of Car Dekho in the past which are expected to analyse what drives a particular car's selling price in the future-Age of vehicle, Brand of the vehicle, KM driven on the vehicle, Vehicle Fuel Type, Vehicle Gear box and Previous Owners
